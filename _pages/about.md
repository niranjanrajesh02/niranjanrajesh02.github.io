I am a PhD student in the [Cognitive Science Department @ UCSD](https://cogsci.ucsd.edu/) where I work with [Meenakshi Khosla](https://cogsci.ucsd.edu/people/faculty/meenakshi-khosla.html) and [Virginia de Sa](https://cogsci.ucsd.edu/people/faculty/virginia-de-sa.html) on understanding neural representations in biological and artificial neural networks. I hope to uncover the algorithms that help us perceive, understand and learn from the world around us.

Previously, I worked in visual neuroscience as a Research Assistant in the [Vision Lab @ IISc](https://sites.google.com/site/visionlabiisc/). Before that, I completed a Bachelor of Science (Hons.) and a postgraduate diploma in Computer Science at [Ashoka University](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/), where I wrote my capstone thesis on understanding the behavior of deep neural networks.

When I am not thinking about how we see in an academic setting, I put the marvel of sight to good use by watching every sitcom in existence.
